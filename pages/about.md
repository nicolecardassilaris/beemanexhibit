## About the Collection

The Beeman Historic Costume Collection is a teaching archive of historical garments dating from the 1870s to the 2010s, including international and children's clothing as well as a bridal collection. The collection is under the direction of Dr. Diana Saiki. The collection was established in the 1930s at Ball State University in Muncie, IN by a professor named Mary Beeman (the head of the Department of Home Economics) when she was gifted a trunk full of 19th century clothing by the Frank C. Ball family. This collection has significant potential for interdisciplinary study which is why students and faculty in the Fashion Studies and History at Ball State University use this teaching collection for hands-on, primary source research and investigative analysis into the construction of clothing, clothing production, and historic fashion trends. Students are also encouraged to explore social norms through the study of historic clothing. The collection is housed in two location at Ball State University in Muncie, Indiana -- the Whitinger Business building and the Applied Technology building.:
